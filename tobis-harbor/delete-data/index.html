Delete your Tobi's Harbor data

Tobi's Harbor has no accounts and no sign-up: we never ask for your name or e-mail. The only data that leaves your phone is anonymous play statistics, and only if you agreed to share them. Here is how to delete everything.

Anonymous play statistics

  1. Open Tobi's Harbor and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data. The game asks Unity Analytics to delete all statistics linked to your game's random install id and turns Data sharing off. Unity usually completes the deletion within 30 days.

To only stop sending statistics, turn off Data sharing in Settings.

Game progress on your phone

Your progress is stored only on your device. Settings → Reset progress deletes it and starts the game over. Uninstalling the game also deletes it (on Android, a copy may remain in your Google system backup if it is turned on).

If you no longer have the game

Without the game we cannot tell which statistics are yours, because they carry only a random install id and no name, e-mail or device identifier. They are deleted automatically when Unity's retention period ends (raw events after at most 1 year). Questions: heyoggi.games@gmail.com.
