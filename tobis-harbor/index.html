Help Tobi bring a sleepy seaside harbor back to life by sorting cargo, clearing shelves and restoring the docks. A cozy puzzle game you can play in a few minutes a day, also offline.

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? E-mail us at heyoggi.games@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the game version (Settings → bottom of the screen). We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. The game has no account and no cloud save, so uninstalling it deletes your progress (on Android, a copy may come back from your Google system backup if it is turned on).

How do I start the game over?

Settings → Reset progress. The game asks you to confirm first. Your sound, language and data-sharing settings are kept.

How do I stop sharing play statistics or delete them?

Settings → turn off Data sharing; from then on no statistics are sent. Settings → Delete my data asks Unity Analytics to delete the anonymous statistics linked to your game; this is usually completed within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
