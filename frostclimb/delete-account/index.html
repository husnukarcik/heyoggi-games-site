FrostclimbPrivacy Policy

Delete your Frostclimb account

Option 1 – In the app (instant)

  1. Open Frostclimb.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account (if you signed in with Apple or Google) or Delete my data (if you play as a guest) at the bottom, then confirm.

Option 2 – By e-mail

Send an e-mail to heyoggi.games@gmail.com with the subject “Frostclimb account deletion”. Include your player name as shown on the leaderboard and the sign-in method you used, if any (Apple or Google, and its e-mail address). We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your game account (Firebase account ID and linked Apple/Google sign-in, if any)
  • Your cloud save and the progress stored on your device: levels, stars, coins, settings and purchased items
  • Your player name and scores on the leaderboard

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us after deletion. Purchase receipts remain with Apple or Google as required for their own records, and advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases, and coins or items bought with it cannot be restored afterwards.

Frostclimb hesabını sil

1. Yol – Uygulamadan (hemen)

  1. Frostclimb’i aç.
  2. Ayarlar (dişli simgesi) → Hesap’a gir.
  3. En alttaki Hesabımı sil’e (Apple veya Google ile giriş yaptıysan) ya da Verilerimi sil’e (misafir olarak oynuyorsan) dokun ve onayla.

2. Yol – E-postayla

heyoggi.games@gmail.com adresine “Frostclimb hesap silme” konulu bir e-posta gönder. Sıralamada görünen oyuncu adını ve varsa giriş yöntemini (Apple veya Google ve e-posta adresi) yaz. Silme 30 gün içinde tamamlanır.

Neler silinir

Oyun hesabın ve bağlı Apple/Google girişi, bulut kaydın (ilerleme, bölümler, yıldızlar, altınlar, ayarlar, satın alınan öğeler), sıralamadaki adın ve skorların. Silmeden sonra bizde hiçbir şey kalmaz. Satın alma makbuzları Apple veya Google’da kalır; silme işlemi satın alımları iptal etmez veya iade etmez.
