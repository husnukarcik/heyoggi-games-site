Delete your Frostclimb account

Option 1 – In the app (instant)

  1. Open Frostclimb.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) → Account.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account (if you signed in with Apple or Google) or Delete my data (if you play as a guest), then confirm.

Option 2 – By e-mail

Send an e-mail to heyoggi.games@gmail.com with the subject “Frostclimb account deletion”. Include your player name as shown on the leaderboard and the sign-in method you used, if any (Apple or Google, and its e-mail address). We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your game account (Firebase account ID and linked Apple or Google sign-in, if any)
  • Your cloud save and the progress stored on your device: levels, stars, coins, settings and purchased items
  • Your player name and scores on the leaderboard

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us after deletion. Purchase receipts remain with Apple or Google for their own records, and advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases, and items bought with it cannot be restored afterwards.
