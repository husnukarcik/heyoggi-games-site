Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems? E-mail us at heyoggi.games@gmail.com. Please include your device model and your player name. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings → Account and sign in with Apple or Google. When you sign in with the same account on the new device, your progress and purchases come back.

I bought something but didn’t receive it.

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used to buy, then restart the game. If it still doesn’t appear, e-mail us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google Play.

How do I change the controls?

Settings → Controls: One hand, Tilt or Classic. In Tilt mode you can also choose the Sensitivity.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Account → Delete my account / Delete my data, or see Delete your account.
