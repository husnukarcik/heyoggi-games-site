Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Open Settings → Account and sign in with Apple or Google. Your progress and purchases are restored when you sign in with the same account on the new device.

I bought something but didn’t receive it.
Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used to buy, restart the game, and e-mail us with the date of the purchase if it still doesn’t appear. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

How do I change the controls?
Settings → Controls: One hand, Tilt or Classic. In Tilt mode you can also set the sensitivity.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Account → Delete my account / Delete my data, or see Delete your account.
