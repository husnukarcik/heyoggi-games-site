Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Frostclimb mobile game (“the app”), published by HeyOggi Games (“we”), collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Game account ID – an anonymous identifier created automatically by Google Firebase Authentication when you first play. Used to save your progress in the cloud and for the leaderboard.
  • Sign-in details (optional) – if you link the game to Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and the e-mail address provided by that service (Apple may give a private relay address). Used to restore your progress and purchases on a new device.
  • Player name (optional) – the name you type in Settings. It is shown next to your score on the public leaderboard. If you don’t set one, a generic name such as “Player 4821” is used.
  • Game data – progress, levels, stars, coins, scores, settings and purchased items. Used to run the game and sync it between your devices. Your best score and player name are visible to other players on the leaderboard.
  • Purchase records – which in-app items you bought, so we can deliver and restore them. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Advertising and device data – collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, ad interactions, and crash/diagnostic data. Used to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. In Tilt mode the app uses the phone’s motion sensor only while you play; this data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent through Google’s consent message, and you can change your choice later. On iOS, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise non-personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. If you buy “Remove Ads”, interstitial ads are no longer shown. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Service providers

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) stores your game account and cloud save; Google AdMob provides ads; Apple and Google provide sign-in and process in-app purchase payments. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

4. Data on your device

Your progress and settings are also stored on your device so that the game works offline. Uninstalling the app removes this copy.

5. How long we keep data

Your cloud save and leaderboard entry are kept until you delete them. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your data in the app at any time: Settings → Account, then Delete my account (if you signed in with Apple or Google) or Delete my data (if you play as a guest). This permanently removes your cloud save, your leaderboard entry and your game account. You can also ask us by e-mail; see Delete your account. E-mail requests are completed within 30 days.

7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to cloud saves is restricted so that each player can only read and change their own data.

8. Children

Frostclimb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be announced in the app.

11. Contact
