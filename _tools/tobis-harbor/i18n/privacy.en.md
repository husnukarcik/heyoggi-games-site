# Tobi's Harbor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Developer: HeyOggi Games

Tobi's Harbor (the "game") is a single-player puzzle game that also works offline. This policy explains what data the game
collects, why, how long it is kept and what rights you have over it.

## 1. Data we collect

**No account, no sign-up.** The game never asks for or collects your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts
or photos.

**Game progress (on your device only).** Stars, gold, lives, completed levels, harbor tasks, settings and tutorial state are
stored in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it (on Android, if system backup is on,
a copy may remain in your Google account backup under Google's privacy policy).

**Anonymous play statistics (only with your consent).** To improve the game, and only after you accept on the first launch
(you can change your choice in Settings at any time), we collect events of these kinds through Unity Analytics (Unity
Technologies, "Unity Gaming Services"):

- app launch, days since install, app version;
- level start and end: level number, outcome (win / lose / quit), moves left, time spent, attempt number, boosters used,
  hints shown;
- changes in in-game currencies (stars, gold, lives) and their reason;
- harbor tasks completed, tutorial steps, hints shown;
- rewarded ad offers shown, started, completed or failed;
- changes to the sound / music / vibration / hint settings.

These events are sent together with a random **install id** generated on your device when the game first opens. It does not
identify you, your device or your accounts in other apps; reinstalling the game creates a new one. Unity Analytics also
processes technical information such as device model, operating system version, country (derived from the IP address, which
is not stored), language and session time. Details: Unity's privacy policy (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) and the Unity
Gaming Services data processing description.

**If you decline**, no statistics are sent at all; the game works just the same.

## 2. Why we use the data

- To see which levels are too hard or too easy and fix the balance.
- To find bugs and places where players get stuck.
- To understand how often the rewarded ad offers are used.

The data is not used to build advertising profiles and is not sold to third parties.

## 4. Retention

- Game progress: on your device until you uninstall the game.
- Anonymous statistics: in Unity Analytics for Unity's standard retention period after collection (raw events up to 1 year;
  aggregated reports may be kept longer).

## 5. Your rights and deletion requests

- **Withdraw consent:** Settings → turn off "Data sharing". No events are sent from then on.
- **Delete your data:** Settings → "Delete my data". The game sends Unity Analytics a request to delete all analytics data
  tied to your install id and turns consent off. Unity normally completes deletion within 30 days.
- To delete local game progress, uninstall the app.
- Questions and requests: **heyoggi.games@gmail.com**

You can exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability granted by the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish
KVKK and similar laws through this address.

## 6. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Analytics runs only
with explicit consent and contains no personal data. If you believe a child's data was collected by mistake, write to the
address above and we will delete it.
